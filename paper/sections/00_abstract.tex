This paper presents \systemname{}, a reproducible post-billing FinOps framework that converts heterogeneous AWS, Azure, and GCP billing exports into canonical cost, allocation, and decision artifacts. The system combines a Unified Cost Allocation Schema, cloud-specific Net Effective Cost (NEC) modeling, shared-cost distribution, deterministic waste detection, structured reasoning over billing signals, auditable recommendation scoring, lightweight month-end forecasting, and lifecycle-aware validation outputs. Evaluated on a synthetic benchmark with 18{,}992 normalized billing rows, the implementation materializes 95 persisted recommendations and achieves forecast backtest error of \$7.86 mean absolute error and 2.98\% mean absolute percentage error. The results show that cross-cloud billing semantics can be normalized into an explainable decision pipeline without relying on proprietary vendor tooling, while also making the current validation boundary explicit: the artifact strongly supports reproducibility, traceability, and internal consistency, but lifecycle realization evidence remains pre-production.
